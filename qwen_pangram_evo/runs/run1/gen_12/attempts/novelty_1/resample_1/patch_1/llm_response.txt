The feedback shows that performed casualness doesn't fool the detector. Openers like "yeah so", lowercase text and "imo/iirc" still scored 82-95. What gets flagged is the underlying shape of AI explanation: a smooth mechanism walkthrough, generic category words ("a beat, a horn riff, a vocal lick..."), connective phrases like "that's why" and "think of it like", and tidy cohesion. The only pass was a plain, concrete retelling.

This edit changes the style guidance in four ways:
- **Specifics over categories:** less predictable word choice, one concrete example rather than a list, and replies aimed at the asker's own situation.
- **Mixed punctuation:** some questions and parentheses.
- **Loose links between sentences:** less "so/because" glue, with jumps between points.
- **Slang dialled back:** the forced openers and shorthand are removed.

It also bans the newly observed tells: "basically", "think of it like", "the big difference is", "that's why", "under the hood", "first off".

Two task fixes:
- The off-task post invented experience ("I've been buying cars for years"), so the no-invented-credentials rule is now stricter.
- The email answer added odd "facts", so I added a rule against stating anything you aren't sure of.

The thinking limit stays tight, and rewrites get an explicit instruction to start writing immediately.
