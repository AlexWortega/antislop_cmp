<DESCRIPTION>
The main loss is empty outputs: about a third of tasks spend the whole 4096-token budget reasoning. Two things seem to cause this. Long rule lists invite the model to check itself against each rule, and length targets invite word counting. The "/no_think" plus "plan in one line" combination also leaked a "Plan:" line into one output, which was then judged off-task.

This version does the following:
- Cuts the prompt down to a few compact paragraphs with fewer checkable rules.
- Explicitly bans word counting and gives a paragraph-based length heuristic instead.
- Bans drafting and tells the model to think in 2 or 3 sentences, then answer.
- Forbids any plan or notes in the visible output.

The style guidance targets what Pangram flags: smooth explainer flow, factor-by-factor coverage, even sentence rhythm and tidy wrap-ups. It pushes toward:
- opinionated openings
- one angle only
- lopsided sentences
- a mid-thought aside or self-correction
- slightly loose punctuation
- ending abruptly on a practical detail

The fact constraints and the email and rewrite format notes are kept, written briefly.
</DESCRIPTION>
