The current prompt had 8 of 25 outputs come back empty. That is the biggest loss: those tasks fail on every metric. The likely cause is that the samples and the long NEVER list push the model to draft a version, compare it to the samples and audit it against the rules inside its reasoning.

This edit makes three changes:
- **Stronger thinking cap at the top.** It spells out that the model must not compare drafts to the samples and must not audit against the rules while thinking. It also says a small rule slip costs less than an empty answer.
- **Short reminder at the very end.** Recency matters for reasoning models, so the cap is repeated just before "Output only the final text."
- **Two style cues for the detector.** Pangram tends to flag smooth, evenly dense explanation. The new cues ask for a concrete everyday example in place of abstract mechanism, and for one small aside or tangent like the ones in the samples. They should lower AI scores without hurting task quality.

The samples stay because they produced the only pass so far.
