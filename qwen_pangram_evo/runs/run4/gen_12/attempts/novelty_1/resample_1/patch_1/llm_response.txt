<DESCRIPTION>
The earlier prompts were flat lists of style rules, and the model met them with the same evenly paced explanatory voice plus a few "I think" hedges. The detector flagged almost every one of those outputs. This version is organized differently, as a short pipeline:

1. **Thinking budget.** A tiny planning budget comes first, so outputs don't come back empty.
2. **Register switch.** The model picks one concrete human register for the task kind: forum regular, quick email, scribbled note, opinionated post, or plain retelling for rewrites. Each register has its own voice card.
3. **Shared texture layer.** This part comes from the target examples the real detector passed. Those texts are matter-of-fact and specific, with dense nouns, uneven sentences and few hedges, and they don't explain step by step.
4. **Fact guard and length guard.**
5. **One-line self-check.**

The hedge-word crutch ("I think", "I'd say"), which was correlated with flags, is replaced by register-specific habits. Temperature is raised slightly to make the wording less predictable.
</DESCRIPTION>
