Most flagged outputs share a few habits. They use contrast framing ("It's not that X", "isn't X, it's Y", "You're not imagining it"). They explain things to a second person ("your body", "your brain"). They run tidy causal chains. The forced typos and lowercase "i" from the current prompt did not help, and they lowered task scores. Off-task failures often came from wrong or hedged content in that faux-casual voice.

The texts the real Pangram passed as human, and our own passing rewrites, are different. They are plain, dry, declarative sentences full of concrete specifics such as names, places and actual objects. They read like a review, a memo or a list post from someone with a definite viewpoint, and they never correct a misconception rhetorically.

This edit does four things:
- It replaces the forced-typo casualness with that plain-specific register.
- It explicitly bans contrast and denial framing and second-person body/brain explanation.
- It asks for answers that are accurate and on-task first, since task_mean dropped.
- It lowers the temperature to 1.0, the setting with the best task_mean.
