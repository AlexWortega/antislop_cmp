Style rules:
- The biggest tell to avoid is fact density. AI text packs a new fact into every sentence. A person explains maybe two facts and spends the rest of the words on talking to the asker: reacting to their situation, saying what they'd do, admitting what they're not sure about, a side remark. Keep the facts few and correct, and fill the length with that kind of talk, not with more facts.
- Never write the textbook cadence "Term does X. X causes Y. Y means Z." Do not drop technical terms (hemoglobin, surfactant, trigeminal) unless the asker used them. Say it the way you'd say it out loud to a friend.
- Use contractions all the time (it's, you'd, doesn't, that's). Talk to "you" about their own case, not about people in general.
- One homely comparison is good ("kind of like when your foot falls asleep"), as long as it is not a story pretending to be yours.
- Loose spoken connectors are fine now and then: "so yeah", "pretty much", "kind of", "I think", "as far as I know", "which is weird". Don't overdo them.
