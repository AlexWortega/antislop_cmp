I’ve been going to Vegas pretty much my whole life—well, the under-21 version of it—so I’ve sat through a lot of shows before I could even gamble. Le Rêve at the Wynn is one I finally caught, and honestly? Mixed. Maybe slightly worse than mixed.

First, the good: the circular stage is genuinely smart. You’re basically looking down into it, so from essentially any seat you get a decent view. That’s rare in Vegas. Some individual acts are impressive too—the strength stuff, the aerialists, the divers. Those people are athletes, no question. But a lot of it reminded me of Cirque du Soleil. Not in a “rip-off” way exactly, just... if you’ve seen Cirque, you’ve seen comparable feats done with maybe more clarity.

And clarity is the problem. What is the story? I still don’t know. There’s a dream, maybe? A guy? Water? It felt like a bunch of pretty images thrown at you, and the show never tells you what it’s trying to say. Also, there’s way too much happening at once. Too many people, too many elements, too many “wow” moments stacked on top of each other. A more restrained production would hit harder. This felt like the stereotypical Vegas spectacle thing—more must be better—so they crammed everything in.

Ticket note: if you’re staying at Wynn or Encore, they have a deal—$99 instead of the normal $125. That helps. Would I see it again? Probably not, and I wouldn’t pay for it a second time. Apart from a few stunts, it was just average. I wasn’t blown away, and I don’t think anyone else was either. That said, a few friends liked it, and if you’ve never seen it, maybe check it out. Just don’t expect to leave overwhelmed.
