The main spectacle is the draw: a pool with platforms that rise and lower, performers swimming, diving, and appearing to walk on water, plus a dive from roughly 25 feet above the pool. If you want an impressive major Vegas show without Cirque du Soleil’s very high prices, I recommend La Rêve as a worthy alternative. We left impressed.
