The space bar rattles. Hit it with the edge of your thumb and it sounds like a loose pen cap in a drawer. The E key also repeats now and then, so "example" becomes "eexample" and I have to backspace. It happens maybe once an afternoon, always when I'm typing fast and not looking.

I paid $38 and kept it anyway, because of the switches. When I asked the seller what they were, the answer took a week. My old membrane board felt like pressing wet cardboard by comparison.

My job is mostly email, often two or three hours in a row, and my wrists don't ache by the end anymore. That mattered more than the rattle.

Sending it back would have meant paying for postage and boxing it up, and I'd have been without a keyboard while I waited. So it stays on the desk, and I tap the space bar a little closer to the middle now.
